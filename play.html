Wabash Flight

Fly anywhere on planet Earth, right in your browser.

Drive anywhere on Earth. Start beside your aircraft at the airport below (press Enter next to it to fly), or search a place to start in a city. Get out of any stopped aircraft with Enter too.

Joystick, yoke, throttle, pedals & gamepad

Plug in any number of controllers (stick + throttle + rudder pedals works). Press a button on each so the browser shows it. To set something up, click Detect and move that control. Your setup is saved for each device. Use Chrome or Edge: Safari on Mac doesn't see most joysticks, and Firefox on Mac can miss slider throttles.

Photorealistic 3D cities

Wabash Flight uses Cesium World Terrain and Google photorealistic 3D cities (real buildings in New York, London, Tokyo…). Turn them off on slower phones and tablets. Optional: paste your own Cesium ion token to use it instead of the built-in one.

First flight: pick the Wabash Trainer at KLAF, hold R for full power, at 55 knots gently hold S to lift the nose, then let go. To land: line up with the runway, reduce power so the green VS number reads about −500, add flaps with V, and raise the nose just before touching down. Aim for a touchdown under 200 ft/min for a “Butter” landing!

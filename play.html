Guns

1 pistol (you start with it) · 2 revolver · 3 sawn-off · 4 SMG · 5 shotgun · 6 assault rifle · 7 hunting rifle · 8 sniper · 9 machine gun · 0 fists / melee · Z cycle. Buy them at Levee Pawn & Loan (red marker downtown).

Cars & bikes

W/S gas / brake & reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake · Shift nitro · C camera · H horn · K siren · R radio. Cars are bought: Tab → 🚗 Vehicles (or Esc → Vehicles). Stealing from traffic still works, but the cops won't like it.

Planes

W/S throttle · A/D roll · ↓ nose up · ↑ nose down · Q/E rudder · F get out (parachute above 12 m).

Helicopter

Everything else

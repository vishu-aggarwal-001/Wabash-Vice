MAP — click anywhere to set a GPS waypoint · drag to pan · scroll to zoom

Golden Gears found: 0/30 — they're hidden on streets and rooftops all over town ($500 each).

Hold T to fast-forward time (day & night cycle only). Your money, gears and settings save automatically in this browser.

On foot

WASD move · Shift run · Space jump · F steal / enter any car: parked cars get broken into, traffic gets carjacked · Mouse look · Scroll zoom · C camera · Click or Q punch, swing or shoot (aim with the mouse) · E kick · 1 fists / melee · 2 pistol · 3 SMG · 4 shotgun · 5 rifle · Z cycle weapons · H use a medkit

Jobs, phone & story

Tab phone: start a job (Police, Taxi, Paramedic, Delivery), call contacts for story missions, buy armor & medkits · X end your shift · Y/N accept / decline a call · stand in a yellow marker and press J for activities

Cars & bikes

W/S gas / brake & reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake (drift) · Shift nitro · Click / Q drive-by shooting (aim with the mouse, 2–5 pick a gun) · H horn · K siren (emergency vehicles) · R radio

Planes

W/S throttle · A/D roll (steer on the ground) · ↓ nose up · ↑ nose down · Q/E rudder · Shift afterburner · Space wheel brakes. Take off: full throttle down the runway, then hold ↓. Exit above 12 m to parachute.

Helicopter

Space climb · Shift or Z descend · W/S tilt forward / back · A/D turn · Q/E strafe. You can land on rooftops.

Boats

W/S throttle · A/D steer · launch from the Wabash at Tapawingo Park.

People

Walk up to anyone and press G to talk: chat, ask for work, ask the way, compliment, insult, or shake them down. Answer with 1–4 or click. Wanted? Try talking to a cop with cash in your pocket. People marked 💬 on the map are contacts with jobs for you (phone → Contacts), and ordinary people sometimes ask for a favour when you ask about work.

Everything else

You live in West Lafayette: your car is in the driveway, and walking up to the 🏠 front door saves the game. M map & GPS · V vehicle spawner · J start mission · T fast-forward time · Esc menu · F1 help overlay. Gamepads work too (triggers to drive, Y to enter/exit).
